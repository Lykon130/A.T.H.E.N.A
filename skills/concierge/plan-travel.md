---
name: plan-travel
department: concierge
stage: human-led
description: Use when the user wants a trip planned or researched — flights, lodging, itinerary — from options through to a bookable plan.
---

1. Clarify dates, destination(s), budget, and constraints. Check `vault/wiki/profile.md` for known travel preferences.
2. Research flight/lodging options, build a day-by-day itinerary draft.
3. Write the plan to `vault/outputs/travel-{destination}-{date}.md`.
4. Same rule as `book-appointment`: this drafts and researches. Actually booking flights/hotels means real payment — that step always needs your explicit action, every time, no exceptions.
