Cross-cutting version of the "Council of High Intelligence" reference — not owned by Strategy, callable from Finance (a big position), Legal (before signing something material), Sales (a major deal term), or anywhere else a decision is big enough to earn it.

1. **Initial answers** — work the question through the lenses that actually apply (pick 2-4, not all): Logic (does the reasoning hold), Strategy (best move), First Principles (strip assumptions), Ethics (consequences), Systems (interactions), Risk (what could go wrong).
2. **Challenge round** — each lens pushes back on the others, surfaces blind spots.
3. **Revision round** — refine based on the challenges.
4. **Synthesis** — one final recommendation, noting what was rejected and why.
5. Write the full deliberation to `vault/raw/council/{date}-{topic}.md`, the synthesis to `vault/outputs/decision-{topic}-{date}.md`.

Reserve this for decisions that matter. `plan` handles daily priorities; department skills handle routine work. This is for the calls worth slowing down for.
