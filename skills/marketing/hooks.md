name: hooks
department: marketing
stage: human-led
description: Use when the user wants hook/headline options for a piece of content, based on research findings or a given topic.
---

1. Read the relevant research from `vault/raw/marketing/research-{date}.md` if available, or take the topic directly from the user.
2. Draft 5-8 hook variations — different angles (curiosity, contrarian, direct benefit, story-open).
3. Write to `vault/outputs/hooks-{topic}-{date}.md`.
4. Hand the chosen hook to `scripts`.
