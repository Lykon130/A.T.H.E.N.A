2. Identify what's performing well and why (angle, format, timing).
3. Write findings to `vault/raw/marketing/research-{date}.md`.
4. Hand off usable ideas to `hooks`.

Note: this is niche/competitor-specific, different scope from News department's `global-scan` (world events) — don't duplicate that work here.
